About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Ye Zheng's lab at The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center. I develop statistical and machine-learning methods for cancer genomics. My current work extracts multiple layers of information, regulatory activity and copy-number alterations, from a single chromatin-profiling assay on archival FFPE tumor tissue, and aligns single-cell and spatial proteomics data across measurement platforms.

I received my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering (statistics track) from the Georgia Institute of Technology in 2024, advised by Yajun Mei and Jianjun Shi. My dissertation developed sequential change-point detection methods for streaming data under sampling control. Before joining MD Anderson I was a Postdoctoral Associate in the School of Global Public Health at New York University (2024–2025). I obtained my B.S. in Statistics from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2019, advised by Zemin Zheng.
